## 🏃 Featured: Runora — [runora.app](https://runora.app)

**Runora is a running-event platform**: runners register for races, get their bib numbers, and receive their finisher kits at home, all from one app.

**What it does**
- 🎟️ **Event registration** in the mobile app and on the web, with shareable event links (`runora.app/e/...`) that open straight into the app
- 🔢 **Bib number delivery** after registration by email, WhatsApp and SMS, with automatic fallback between channels
- 🔐 **OTP sign-in** that tries WhatsApp first and falls back to SMS or email
- 📦 **Finisher kit delivery** through courier partners, with a courier-status webhook that marks each kit as delivered automatically
- 🌍 **Localisation**: Bangla (রানোরা) for users in Bangladesh, English elsewhere
- 🗺️ **Maps** for routes and events

**Where it's heading**
- ⌚ Apple Watch app for mirrored and standalone run recording, with Runora watch faces
- 🔜 Wear OS, Garmin and Huawei support, since many of our runners use Garmin
- 🎮 An in-app running game, built into Runora rather than as a separate app

**My role:** I design and build the whole product: the Flutter app (Android and iOS), the backend API, the web registration flow, messaging integrations and courier webhooks.
